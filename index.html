About

Welcome to The Nestled Outpatient, a compassionate addiction recovery center located in Las Vegas, Nevada, where healing begins with a structured, evidence-based approach to outpatient care that builds on the foundation of residential treatment. Situated at 7040 Laredo St in the heart of Las Vegas, this dedicated facility offers a seamless continuum of care for individuals stepping down from inpatient rehab or seeking intensive support in their community. The Nestled Outpatient combines clinical expertise with genuine warmth, creating a safe and nurturing environment where clients can rebuild their lives with confidence and lasting sobriety. Whether you're transitioning out of a residential program or beginning your recovery journey, their experienced team is here to guide every step forward.

Services

Partial Hospitalization Program (PHP)

Provides intensive, structured daily treatment ideal for clients stepping down from inpatient rehabilitation.

Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP)

Offers flexible yet comprehensive therapy sessions that support recovery while clients return to daily life.

Dual Diagnosis Treatment

Simultaneously addresses co-occurring mental health disorders alongside substance use for integrated, lasting healing.

Individual Therapy

Delivers personalized one-on-one counseling to explore root causes of addiction and build coping strategies.

Group Therapy

Facilitates peer-supported therapeutic sessions that foster connection, accountability, and shared growth in recovery.

Family Therapy

Engages loved ones in the healing process to rebuild trust, improve communication, and strengthen the client's support system.
